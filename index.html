About

Research
grounded in
lived realities

I am a campaign strategist, qualitative researcher, ethnographer, and policy advocate working at the intersection of gender justice, human rights, and community-led change. My work spans academic research, government engagement, and programme delivery across Northeast India, South India, and the United Kingdom.

My research is published in peer-reviewed journals including the International Journal of Asian Christianity and the Intercultural Human Rights Law Review, and has been presented at the BSA Sociology of Religion Annual Conference at the University of Bristol.

I have led campaigns featured on NDTV, Times of India, Business Standard, and Vogue India, and have been recognised by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation for my work on menstrual health and women's entrepreneurship in Northeast India.

I hold an MA in Social Work (Community Organisation & Development Practice) from Tata Institute of Social Sciences and a BA in Sociology (Honours) from the University of Delhi.
